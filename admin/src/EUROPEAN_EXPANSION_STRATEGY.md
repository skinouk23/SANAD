## 8. ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

### 8.1 Risk Matrix

|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Doctolib enters home care | Medium | High | Speed to market, tech moat, brand loyalty |
| Regulatory barriers | Medium | High | Local legal teams, proactive compliance |

| Technology failures | Low | High | Redundancy, security audits, monitoring |
| Economic downturn | Medium | Medium | Essential services, diversified revenue |
| Talent acquisition | Medium | Medium | Competitive packages, remote options |
| Currency fluctuations | Low | Low | Multi-currency treasury, hedging |

### 8.2 Competitive Response Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Doctolib Launches Home Care**

*Probability: 40% within 3 years*

**Our Response:**
- Accelerate market penetration
- Deepen provider relationships
- Emphasize technology advantages (AI, tracking)
- Highlight commission vs subscription model
- Consider strategic partnership or acquisition discussions

**Scenario 2: New Well-Funded Competitor**

*Probability: 60% within 3 years*

**Our Response:**
- Build network effects quickly
- Secure exclusive partnerships
- Invest in technology moat
- Consider M&A of smaller competitors
- Focus on customer experience excellence

**Scenario 3: Insurance Companies Build In-House**

*Probability: 30% within 5 years*

**Our Response:**
- Position as technology partner, not competitor
- White-label offerings for insurers
- B2B2C model development
- API-first architecture for integration

### 8.3 Contingency Plans

**Plan A: Aggressive Growth (Base Case)**
- Full European expansion as planned
- Series B at month 24
- IPO track by year 5

**Plan B: Focused Growth**
- Concentrate on Germany + France only
- Achieve profitability before expansion
- Smaller Series B, delayed Series C
- Strategic sale option at year 4-5

**Plan C: Strategic Partnership**
- Partner with major insurer or hospital network
- Accelerated growth through partner distribution
- Shared economics model
- Potential acquisition path
